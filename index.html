What Makes a Great Fitness Website

Surprisingly, you don't need too many features to get more memberships. Some simple things can help make a hesitant person feel confident enough to join your facility right away.

A Real Good Reason to Start Immediately

As said before, don't make your homepage/landing page all about your gym's history or equipment. Give a good offer and make it visible immediately.

A Schedule That Feels Like It’s Part of Your Website

Since your schedule is one of the most important pages on the site, it shouldn't look like you've added another company's software to your design.

A Pricing Section That Helps People Decide Easily

Just listing prices isn’t enough. Prepare it carefully so visitors can easily and quickly understand which plans might work best for them.

Instructors People Can Get to Know

Friendly, enthusiastic fitness instructors are always highly appreciated. Your instructor profiles should show their personalities, specialties, philosophies, and their preferred types of members.

Photos & Videos that Capture the Real Experience

The best idea is to show what a normal session looks like: the members, the coaching, the energy, the equipment, the space, the music and lighting systems… and the list goes on.

The Whole Thing Seems Intimate & Welcoming

There’s no single formula to make it happen. It’s a mixture of a wonderful design, great content, high site performance, and small, thoughtful details.

Member Transformation Stories on Every Page

This helps increase your conversion rate because people feel encouraged when they see that others have already achieved what they’re aspiring to achieve, and that it’s actually possible.

Virtual Class Library if You Have Online Offerings

If you don’t want to lose those at-home leads not ready to join yet, you better add on-demand or hybrid programs on your website.
